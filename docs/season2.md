# Season 2: Wheels & Heat

Why: 563 people opened the game; 8 were holders; 96% of guests never came back; 10 Chogs ever robbed, one Chog did 190 of ~280 robberies. The only game is the vault, the live part needs people online at the same time, and between robberies there's nothing to do.

What changes: three new games, the city gets cars and heat, and everything feeds the same three things: **your cash, your rank, your gang's turf**. Holders are the only real players (guests can look around and try the practice vault, as now).

One rule across everything: **your Chog's powers come from its traits, as now, and each power does something fitting in each game.** Your Chog plays the same way everywhere, and no two Chogs play the same.

What other games taught us (sources at the bottom): win on territory, not kills, so anyone helps (Splatoon); matches that swing in the last seconds; escapes with more than one way out and guards you can read, but short, because long routine is what critics hated in The Escapists; fights won by knocking someone out of the ring with a few simple moves (Smash); heat that climbs in steps and that you can shake off by hiding (GTA).

---

## Fight Night

A basement ring under a bar. Two Chog cards on their round bases, sumo style: **knock the other one out of the ring**.

- **No health bar.** Every hit adds to a number on your base (0%, 40%, 120%...). The higher it is, the further the next hit sends you flying. A Chog at 150% can be sent out by one good hit, so a fight is never over until it's over.
- **Three buttons:** hit, dash, and your Chog's power. On a phone: stick on the left, three buttons on the right.
- **Best of three falls,** about 2 minutes a fight. The ring shrinks in the last 15 seconds of a fall, so nobody can run forever.
- **Powers in the ring:** zap = a short laser push from range; tackle = a charging shoulder; hide = dodge through a hit; scare = the other side's controls flip for a second; blink = short teleport; magnet = pull them in; bribe = the crowd throws a bottle at them; sprint = fast feet; hack, distract and lockpick get ring versions too.
- **Alone:** tonight's card. Five bot fighters, each one harder, then the champion, played by the computer as the current champion's own Chog.
- **With people:** challenge anyone in the city. Everyone in the bar watches live and can bet cash on the winner.
- **The belt:** most wins against real players this week. The champion's card hangs over the ring and is the last fight on everyone's card.

## Turf War

Every block of the city between the streets is turf. Win the block and it turns your gang's colour on the map, with your gang's tags on its walls.

- **Start one:** walk onto a block your gang doesn't hold and spray the wall. A war on that block starts in 30 seconds. Everyone in both gangs gets a ping (Telegram, if linked). Bots fill the empty spots, so it runs even if nobody comes.
- **Two minutes, 4 against 4,** on that block's real layout (its alleys, stalls, cars). You spray paint on the ground. **The gang that covers more of the block wins**, not the one that splats more Chogs.
- **Paint is your road:** you move fast on your own colour, slowly on theirs, and your spray refills standing on your own colour.
- **Splat someone** and they drop their spray and go back to their start for 3 seconds.
- **Last 30 seconds:** paint spreads twice as far. Wars are won at the end.
- **Powers set your spray:** zap = a long thin line; sprint = a roller that paints where you walk; tackle = a wide short blast; scare = paint bombs you throw; and so on.
- **What a block is worth:** shown on the map and in the weekly turf ranking. (Whether held blocks also pay the gang's vault is an economy change: ask first.)

## Jailbreak

Busted means 1 hour plus 1 hour per star in jail. Now you can break out.

- **A short run, not a routine:** at most 3 minutes, from your cell to the outside of the wall, at night.
- **Four ways out, every time:**
  - **The vent:** a slow, quiet crawl.
  - **The laundry truck:** it leaves on a timer, so hide in a cart, and guards check carts at random.
  - **Over the wall:** cross the yard between sweeping searchlights.
  - **The tunnel:** you dig a bit on each try, and it stays dug unless a guard finds it.
- **Guards you can read:** they walk set rounds with a light cone, and turn toward noise.
- **Powers:** zap cuts a bar, hide = the light doesn't see you, bribe = one guard looks away, lockpick opens a door, blink skips a fence, scare freezes a guard...
- **Other inmates are real:** jailed holders are in the same jail at the same time. Start a fight in the yard to pull the guards while your friend runs. With 3 or more inmates you can start a riot.
- **Caught:** back to the cell, 10 minutes added, try again after a short wait. **Out:** you come out behind the jail with 1★ heat and it's announced. Your gang can still break you out from outside, as now, with a car waiting by the wall once cars exist.

## The city: cars and heat

- **Cars:** jack any parked car or taxi and drive it. Getaways can be done in a car. Crashing into a stall or a hydrant smashes it.
- **Heat, 1 to 5 stars:** robbing, ramming and escaping all raise it.
  - 1★: cops on foot.
  - 2★: a cop car.
  - 3★: cop cars and roadblocks ahead of you.
  - 4★: spike strips.
  - 5★: a helicopter spotlight sweeping the map.

  Heat drops if nobody sees you: hide in an alley, or swap cars where no cop is looking. Any rival holder can ram or tackle you to knock your bag loose.
- **The Armored Truck:** every day at a fixed time, a cash truck drives a route through the city for 10 minutes. Ram it to a stop, crack the back doors, grab the bags, and run at 5★. Telegram warns 10 minutes before.
- **Out on the street:**
  - Street race: checkpoints across the city against the record holder's ghost.
  - Chog cab: drive strolling Chogs across town before the timer runs out.
  - Secure the bag: one bag of cash in the city; whoever holds it earns, and everyone hunts them.
  - Wreck it: 60 seconds to smash the street.

## Rank and jobs

- **Rank over your Chog's head,** seen by everyone, tied to the token: **Paper hands → Degen → Nad → Whale → Chug**. You earn it from every game (escapes, fights won, blocks taken). Getting busted knocks some off.
- **The Fixer:** three jobs a day that send you across the games, for example "take a block from whoever robbed you last", "break out in under 90 seconds" or "knock out a Whale in the ring". Each one pays cash and rank.
- **Telegram alerts:** link your Telegram once. The bot tells you when your gang's vault is being robbed, your Chog is jailed, a war starts on your gang's block, or someone challenges you to a fight, with a button that drops you right there.

## Promised on the Chogathon site ("keep building", owner, 2026-11)

1. **Monthly seasons.** Gang vaults reset each month. The richest gang, the top robber and the best guard get a title written onto their Chogs for good, and it stays with the Chog when sold. (This replaces the rank idea above: titles plus a career, tied to the token.)
2. **Crew theft.** 2 to 4 Chogs from one gang rob a vault together: one on the lock, one on the safe, one runs the bag. The biggest vaults open only to crews. (Fits with the Armored Truck.)
3. **Vault upgrades paid in $CHOG,** each one a burn: more guards, harder locks, faster police.
4. **Raid alerts on your phone.** An installable app (iPhone and Android) that pings you when your gang's vault is being robbed, so you can jump in and defend. Telegram too.
5. **Chog records on Monad.** Season titles and careers (cash stolen, escapes, catches) in a contract, so marketplaces, bots and other games can read them.
6. **Events every month.** Halloween was 27 Oct; a **winter heist in December**; gang-vs-gang events like Left vs Right.
7. **Community.** The biggest heists and a daily recap on Telegram and Discord, and Discord roles that match your gang.

Plus more games for different tastes: Fight Night (action), Turf War (team), Jailbreak (stealth), street races (cars).

## Build order

1. **Fight Night.** Built; live once the owner says.
2. **Raid alerts:** the installable app with push notifications, and Telegram.
3. **Monthly seasons and titles on the Chog,** with the career record; Season 1 starts on a date the owner picks.
4. **Winter heist,** for December.
5. **Crew theft and the Armored Truck.**
6. **Vault upgrades in $CHOG.**
7. **Turf War, then Jailbreak.**
8. **Cars and heat,** and the street games.
9. **Chog records on Monad** (a contract deploy: owner's OK and gas first).
10. **Discord roles by gang.**

## Ask before building

- Season reset rules and the start date; titles and who counts as "best guard".
- $CHOG prices for each vault upgrade.
- The Monad records contract: deploying costs gas and needs signing.
- What a Fight Night win pays.

- Turf paying gang vaults (economy).
- Bets in Fight Night: limits and the house cut (economy).
- Jailbreak: time added when caught (rule).
- Heat: how much cash a rival takes by knocking your bag loose (economy).
