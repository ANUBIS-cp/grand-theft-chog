# Season 2 art: prompts to make in ChatGPT

Pictures for the new games and the cars, in the order they're needed (Fight Night is built first). You can do them a few at a time. Each game keeps a simple drawn stand-in until its pictures are in.

## How to

1. Use the same ChatGPT chat as before (or a new one). Upload your **`style.png`** first and say: "Same art style as this picture for everything I ask next."
2. Send the prompts one per message. If one drifts from the style, upload `style.png` again and add "Same art style as this picture."
3. Download each as PNG, name it **exactly** as shown, and put it in **`~/chog-city/art-in/season2/`**.
4. Tell me when some are in. I cut, size and fit them, check them in the game at phone and desktop size, and send you screenshots before anything goes live.

Two kinds of picture:

- **Things** on **solid magenta (#FF00FF)**, so I can cut them out.
- **Textures** that fill the whole picture edge to edge and repeat.

## The style (replace [STYLE] in every prompt)

> Style: same polished 2D cartoon game art as the reference picture. Bold, clean, dark navy outlines of even thickness. Flat colours with one soft shade and one small highlight per surface. Chunky, slightly rounded, playful shapes that read clearly at small size. Night-time city mood: warm lights, rich but not neon-saturated colours. No text, letters or numbers.

For **things**, add: "Background: one solid flat magenta colour (#FF00FF) filling the whole picture, with no ground, no shadow and no gradient. Never use magenta or pink-purple on the subject itself."

For **textures**, add: "A seamless, tileable texture seen straight from above, filling the whole square edge to edge with no border, no vignette and no lighting gradient, so it repeats cleanly."
