# City art: prompts to make in ChatGPT

23 pictures turn the code-drawn city into drawn art: the 16 gang buildings, the jail, the practice vault and 5 street things. The game uses each picture as soon as it's in, and keeps the code drawing for anything not made yet, so you can do them a few at a time.

## How to

1. Open ChatGPT and start **one new chat** for all of these. Staying in one chat keeps the style the same across the pictures.
2. Send **Picture 0** first. Make it again until you love the look. It's the style guide for everything after.
3. Then send the prompts below, one per message. If a picture starts to drift from the style, upload Picture 0 again and add: "Same art style as this picture."
4. Download each picture as PNG and name it **exactly** as shown, for example `gang-royals.png`. Put it in the folder `~/chog-city/art-in/`.
5. Tell me when some are in. I clean them up (cut the background off, trim, size), check them in the game and put them live.

**Rules that matter** (already in every prompt):

- **Background:** solid magenta (#FF00FF). That's how the background gets cut off cleanly. If ChatGPT makes a see-through PNG instead, that works too.
- **No ground or shadow under the subject:** the game draws its own.
- **View:** from the front and above, so the roof and the whole front wall show. Buildings have their **door in the middle of the front wall**, where you walk in.
- **No words** unless a prompt asks for them. The game writes the gang names itself.

Pinterest pictures can't be used: almost all of them belong to someone else, and the game would be using their art without permission. Everything here is either made for us or under a licence that allows it (the emoji are Microsoft's Fluent Emoji, MIT licence).

## The style (part of every prompt)

> Style: polished 2D cartoon game art for a top-down multiplayer party game. Bold, clean, dark navy outlines of even thickness. Flat colours with one soft shade and one small highlight per surface. Chunky, slightly rounded, playful shapes that read clearly at small size. Night-time city mood: warm glowing windows, rich but not neon-saturated colours. Camera: three-quarter top-down view from the front, looking down at about 45 degrees, so the roof and the whole front wall are visible; straight-on, no perspective tilt left or right. Background: one solid flat magenta colour (#FF00FF) filling the whole picture, with no ground, no floor, no shadow and no gradient. Never use magenta or pink-purple on the subject itself. No text, letters or numbers unless asked for.
