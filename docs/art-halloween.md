# Halloween art: prompts to make in ChatGPT

14 pictures for Halloween: the haunted vault (its floors, walls and furniture), the phantom, the Blood Moon and the pumpkins. Everything else (ghost guards, the lantern light, the red mist, the animations) I do in the game around these pictures. The first Blood Moon is **27 Oct, 18:00 UTC**, so these are needed by the **26th** to fit and check them in time.

## How to

1. Use the **same ChatGPT chat** you made the buildings in (or a new one). Upload your **`style.png`** (Picture 0 from the buildings) first and say: "Same art style as this picture for everything I ask next."
2. Send the prompts below, one per message. If one drifts from the style, upload `style.png` again and add "Same art style as this picture."
3. Download each as PNG, name it **exactly** as shown, and put it in **`~/chog-city/art-in/halloween/`**.
4. Tell me when some are in. I cut, size and fit them, make the textures repeat without seams, check everything in the game at phone and desktop size, and send you screenshots before anything goes live.

**Two kinds of picture here:**

- **Textures** (floors and walls) fill the **whole** picture edge to edge, no magenta, no border: they repeat across the vault floor.
- **Things** (furniture, the phantom, the moon, the pumpkins) are on **solid magenta (#FF00FF)** like the buildings, so I can cut them out.

## The style (part of every prompt)

> **[STYLE]** Style: same polished 2D cartoon game art as the reference picture. Bold, clean, dark navy outlines of even thickness. Flat colours with one soft shade and one small highlight per surface. Chunky, slightly rounded shapes that read clearly at small size. Haunted Halloween night mood: cold moonlit blues, slate greys and ghostly mint-green, with warm candle-orange as the only warm colour. Spooky but charming, never gory: no blood, no gore. Camera: three-quarter top-down view, looking down at about 45 degrees. No text, letters or numbers.

For **things**, add: "Background: one solid flat magenta colour (#FF00FF) filling the whole picture, with no ground, no shadow and no gradient. Never use magenta or pink-purple on the subject itself."

For **textures**, add: "A seamless, tileable texture seen straight from above, filling the whole square edge to edge with no border, no vignette and no lighting gradient, so it repeats cleanly."

## What I do with them (in the game)

- **Haunted vault:** the floors, walls and furniture swap for these, so it's a different place, not the same vault in the dark. Cobwebs go in the corners, candles along the walls (their light cuts through the dark), and the guards become ghost guards: see-through versions of the gang's own Chogs with glowing eyes.
- **The phantom** floats, bobs, stretches as it drifts through walls, fades in and out, and leaves a trail of mist.
- **Blood Moon in the city:** no red filter over the screen. The night stays as it is; the street lamps burn crimson, a low red mist drifts along the streets, and the moon picture shows whole in the corner badge and reflected in the plaza fountain.
- **Pumpkins** pop out of the street, bob, and burst into coins when someone grabs one.
